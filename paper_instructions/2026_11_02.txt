Modeling

  Standing conventions
    - Always maintain arrival curves + t_20 statistics
    - Unless otherwise stated... maintain 5% 3d molecules
    - Unless otherwise stated... use 1s dwell time

  1. What is the effect of confinement?
     - Sweep (3d/2d) ratio vs effective diffusion rate (no receptors)
     - Sweep (3d/2d) ratio vs t_20 capture rate in ring 1,2,3,4
       (no receptors except in target ring)
     - Sweep 0.1% -> 100% in 10 steps

  2. What is the effect of confinement dwell time?
     - Sweep dwell time vs effective diffusion rate
     - Sweep dwell time vs t_20 capture rate in ring 1,2,3,4
       (no receptors except in target ring)
     - Sweep 10ms -> 1000s in 10 steps

  3. What is the effect of receptor density?
     - Compare (3d/2d) vs (3d); sweep across receptor number; measure t_20
       in ring 1,2,3,4 (no receptors except in target ring)
     - Sweep 10 -> 100000 in 10 steps

  4. What is the effect of capture efficiency?
     - Compare (3d/2d) vs (3d); sweep across capture efficiency; measure t_20
       in ring 1,2,3,4 (no receptors except in target ring)
     - Sweep 0.1% -> 100% in 10 steps

  5. Pseudo-FGF vs pseudo-HH
     - Compare (high 3d) vs (low 3d); sweep across capture efficiency; measure
       t_20 in ring 1,2,3,4 (no receptors except in target ring)
     - Sweep 0.1% -> 100% in 10 steps
